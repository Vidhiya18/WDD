ABOUT HAPPY PAWS

Happy Paws Animal Shelter is dedicated to rescuing abandoned, injured, and homeless animals. We provide medical care, nutrition, shelter, and rehabilitation before helping every animal find a safe and loving forever home.


OUR MISSION OUR VISION
To rescue, protect, and rehabilitate animals while encouraging responsible pet adoption. A world where every animal has a safe home, proper care, and unconditional love.


OUR SERVICES

Service Description
Animal Rescue Rescuing abandoned and injured animals.
Medical Care Vaccination, treatment, and health monitoring.
Shelter & Care Providing food, shelter, and daily care.
Pet Adoption Helping animals find responsible families.
Community Awareness Educating people about animal welfare.
